PortalVault is your go-to collectors iOS app for your Toys-To-Life figures and accessories! 
Built from the ground up with SwiftUI, PortalVault will bring your collection to LIFE! It supports the biggest titles in the industry, including big hits like Skylanders, LEGO Dimensions, and Nintendo's amiibo.

BRING YOUR COLLECTION TO LIFE!

Adding figures to your new digital collection has never been easier!
Simply tap the “Add Figure” button, select the type of scan you want to do (NFC or Portal Scan), tap your figure to the back your iPhone (LEGO Dimensions figures and amiibo only) or a use a Skylanders Trap Team iOS Portal, (required to add Skylanders), and boom! The figure is added automatically to your collection!

SCAN TYPES!

PortalVault has two scan modes. NFC Tap, which uses the built-in iPhone NFC Reader, and Portal Scan, which uses a custom-made SDK called PortalSDK and Bluetooth Low Energy to connect to a real Skylanders Trap Team iOS Portal. A real Skylanders Trap Team iOS Portal is required to use Portal Scan.

PortalVault can read the following types of figures through NFC Tap:

* amiibo
* LEGO Dimensions figures

PortalVault can also read the following types of figures through a Skylanders Trap Team iOS Portal (accessory required):

* Skylanders
* Disney Infinity figures  (Support coming soon!)
